Cambrian Competitive Tournament 2026

Competitive pickleball. Community spirit. Sportsmanship.

Welcome

For the past several years, our neighborhood pickleball tournaments have been built around community, inclusiveness, and fun. We have intentionally created opportunities for players of all skill levels to enjoy meaningful games, meet new people, and strengthen the friendships that make our pickleball community special. That spirit remains at the heart of everything we do.

This tournament introduces a new format for our community—a purely competitive event. It is important to emphasize that this is not replacing our regular community tournaments. Those tournaments will continue to be an integral part of our pickleball community, and the next organizing team has already been identified and is actively planning the next community tournament.

The objective of this event is to provide a platform for players who are looking for a more competitive experience to challenge themselves and enjoy tournament-style play. While the focus shifts toward competition, the values that define our community do not. We expect every participant to compete with integrity, respect their opponents and officials, and demonstrate the sportsmanship that has always made our group welcoming and enjoyable.

We hope this tournament adds another dimension to our community by offering both the excitement of competition and the camaraderie that brings us together on the court.

Rules & Guidance

Unlike our previous community tournaments, this event does not involve organizers creating teams or assigning partners. Since this is a doubles tournament, participants are free to choose their own partner and register together as a doubles pair.

Two Divisions

Doubles Open

Advanced

Open to all players. Players are encouraged to choose this division if they feel it provides the most competitive experience for their pair.

Doubles Open

Intermediate

Open to all players. Players are encouraged to choose this division if they feel it provides the most competitive experience for their pair.

Registration

  • Open to all players from the WhatsApp PB Pickup group.
  • Players register as a doubles pair.
  • Players are free to register in either division based on where they feel they can compete most appropriately.
  • Players who have not yet found a partner are welcome to register as individuals. To help facilitate partner matching, we will publish a list of players who have registered individually. Players can then connect with one another and form a doubles pair based on their preferred partner and playing level.

No Sandbagging

To ensure fair and competitive matchups, sandbagging will not be permitted. Organizers may review registrations and pairings across categories, and if two players who are clearly playing at an Advanced level register together in the Intermediate category, they may be asked to compete in the Advanced category instead.

The goal is to maintain a competitive and enjoyable experience for everyone while keeping the category selection as open and flexible as possible.

Competitive Group Play

Depending on the number of registrations, pairs may be divided into groups, with round-robin play within each group. Not every pair will necessarily play every other pair in the category. Based on the group-stage results, the top-performing pairs will advance to the playoffs and compete for the championship.

Cambrian DUPR Ratings

All matches played in this tournament will contribute to the players' Cambrian DUPR ratings.

Choose Your Competitive Level

Players are encouraged to select the category where they believe they can have the most competitive and enjoyable matches. Choosing the right level will give each pair the best opportunity to compete strongly, advance through the tournament, and ultimately contend for the trophy.

Category Review

Once registration closes, we will publish the registered pairs in each category and open a brief window for participants to finalize their preferred category. This will allow everyone to review the matchups and choose the level of competition in which they would most like to participate.

Final Tournament Format

The final tournament format—including pools, brackets, round-robin structure, and playoff qualification—will be determined after registration closes and will depend on the number of entries in each category. Our goal is to create a competitive, fair, and enjoyable tournament experience for everyone.

Registration Fees

Registration fees for each category will be finalized after registration closes and will depend on the total number of participants registered.

This approach ensures that tournament expenses, including court reservations, balls, prizes, and other event costs, are shared fairly among participants.
